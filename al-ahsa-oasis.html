Al-Ahsa Oasis: Two Million Palms & Ancient Springs

The largest natural desert oasis on Earth, where artesian springs meet golden sand dunes.

Fed by 280 artesian springs, Al-Ahsa sustains 2.5 million date palms across 85 square kilometers of eastern Arabia.

Beside the groves, Jabal Al-Qarah offers naturally cool limestone slot canyons and traditional pottery workshops.

“For six millennia, artesian springs have nourished continuous palm cultivation beside the desert dunes.”
