AlUla & Hegra: The Nabataean Sandstone Sanctuary

Monumental rock-cut necropolises and ancient caravan inscriptions in northwest Arabia.

Hegra preserves 111 monumental Nabataean tombs carved into golden sandstone outcrops in the 1st century CE.

Nearby canyons shelter the cliffside inscriptions of Jabal Ikmah and 12th-century AlUla Old Town.

“Carved from the crown downward, Hegra’s sandstone façades remain crisp after two millennia.”
