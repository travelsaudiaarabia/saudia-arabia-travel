Saudi Coffee (Qahwa) & Dates: Intangible Cultural Heritage

Cardamom-infused Khawlani mountain Arabica poured from brass dallahs beside regional dates.

Saudi Coffee (Qahwa) is lightly roasted from Jazan Khawlani beans and brewed with green cardamom, saffron, and cloves.

Poured from a curved brass dallah into finjan cups, it is always served with fresh regional Ajwa or Sukkari dates.

“A gentle side-to-side tilt of your empty finjan cup signals that your measure of coffee is complete.”

Field Highlights & Landmarks

01

Regional Spice Profiles

Golden saffron roasts in central Najd, clove accents in Hejaz, and ginger notes in the south.

02

Finjan Pouring Etiquette

Cups are poured one-third full so the porcelain rim remains cool and hospitality stays continuous.

03

Regional Date Pairings

Unsweetened spiced coffee paired with Madinah Ajwa, Al-Ahsa Khalas, or Qassim Sukkari dates.
