Asir Highlands & Rijal Almaa: Above the Emerald Clouds

Misty juniper peaks at 3,000 meters and stone fortresses inlaid with white quartz.

Rising 3,000 meters along the Sarawat Escarpment, the Asir Highlands cloak juniper forests in cool mountain mist.

Below the pass, Rijal Almaa preserves 60 stone towers adorned with UNESCO-listed Al-Qatt Al-Asiri frescoes.

“While summer warms the desert plains, Abha’s green juniper ridges rest in 20°C mountain clouds.”
