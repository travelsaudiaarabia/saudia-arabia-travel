Diriyah & At-Turaif: Birthplace of the First Saudi State

Traditional Najdi mud-brick palaces and palm overlooks along Wadi Hanifah.

Founded in the 15th century, At-Turaif showcases monumental Najdi architecture built from sun-baked clay and tamarisk timber.

Across the wadi, Bujairi Terrace offers twilight views of Salwa Palace beside shaded date palm groves.

“Triangular geometric wall openings channel cool valley breezes through multi-story adobe palaces.”
