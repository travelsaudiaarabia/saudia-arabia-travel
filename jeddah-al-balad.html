Jeddah & Al-Balad: Coral Architecture & Red Sea Gateway

Fossilized coral-stone merchant houses crowned with carved teakwood Roshan balconies.

Founded in the 7th century CE, Historic Jeddah (Al-Balad) is constructed from Red Sea coral stone and mangrove timber.

Towering merchant homes use ornate wooden Roshan louvers to draw cool sea breezes through shaded lanes.

“Carved from marine coral limestone and Java teakwood, Al-Balad’s towers act as natural wind-catchers.”
