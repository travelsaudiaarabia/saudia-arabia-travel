The Red Sea & Umluj: Pristine Archipelagos & Coral Reefs

Ninety barrier islands, fringing coral gardens, and turquoise sandbars from Umluj to Al Wajh.

The Al Wajh Bank between Umluj and Duba shelters 90 untouched barrier islands inside crystal-clear turquoise lagoons.

Over 300 species of resilient hard and soft corals thrive just meters beneath the warm coastal surface.

“Seventy-five percent of the Red Sea archipelago’s 90 islands are preserved as marine sanctuaries.”
