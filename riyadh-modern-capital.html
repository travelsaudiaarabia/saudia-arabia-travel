Riyadh: Skybridges, Souqs & Contemporary Najdi Culture

Three-hundred-meter glass towers paired with historic mud-brick forts and traditional markets.

Riyadh blends contemporary glass skyscrapers and metro boulevards with preserved 19th-century desert fortifications.

Tour Al-Masmak Fortress and Souq Al-Zal in the morning before ascending the Kingdom Centre Sky Bridge at dusk.

“From antique brass dallahs in Souq Al-Zal to panoramic sunset views 300 meters above the capital.”
