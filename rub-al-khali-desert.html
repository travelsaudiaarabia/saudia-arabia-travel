Uruq Bani Ma’arid UNESCO (2023) · Best Window: December – February

Rub’ al Khali: Expedition into the Empty Quarter Dunes

The world’s largest contiguous sand sea, home to 250-meter star dunes and Arabian oryx.

Spanning 650,000 square kilometers, the Rub’ al Khali forms the planet’s largest continuous ocean of red quartz dunes.

On its western edge, UNESCO-listed Uruq Bani Ma’arid protects free-roaming herds of white Arabian oryx and gazelles.

“Silence on the crest of a 250-meter star dune is broken only by shifting desert sands.”
