Saudi eVisa, Seasons & High-Speed Rail: Visitor Essentials

Instant eVisa eligibility, 300 km/h Haramain High-Speed Railway links, and seasonal routing.

Citizens of 66+ nations and US, UK, or Schengen visa holders qualify for a one-year multiple-entry Saudi Tourist eVisa.

The electric Haramain High-Speed Railway connects Jeddah and Madinah at 300 km/h, while SAR trains link Riyadh and Al-Ahsa.
