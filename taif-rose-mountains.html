Taif & Al Hada: The City of Roses & Mountain Escarpments

Three hundred million fragrant Damask roses harvested at dawn across high granite valleys.

Perched at 1,879 meters in the Hejaz Mountains, Taif blooms each spring with three hundred million pink Damask roses.

Over 900 terraced mountain farms harvest blossoms at dawn for hydro-distillation in traditional copper alembics.

“Forty thousand hand-picked mountain blossoms distill into a single vial of pure Taif rose otto.”
